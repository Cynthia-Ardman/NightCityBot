# Gun Properties Guide

So you want to buy a gun? Cool. Before you start shopping, here's a quick rundown of how guns are classified on the server. Every weapon has three properties that matter: **Power Level**, **Type**, and **Restriction**. Each one affects what you can buy, how easy it is to find, and what hoops you might need to jump through.

---

## Power Level

Every gun is rated as **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** power. Think of it as the quality tier of the weapon.

| Power Level | What It Means |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Low** | Everyday, reliable firearms. Nothing fancy, but they get the job done. These are your starter guns and common sidearms. |
| **Medium** | A step up in firepower and craftsmanship. Better performance, better build quality. |
| **High** | Top-of-the-line hardware. Military-grade, cutting-edge, or otherwise exceptional weapons. |

### How Power Level Affects Availability

Gun stores get their stock from a weekly wholesale rotation. The system uses a weighted random selection to decide what shows up, and the weights heavily favor lower-tier guns:

- **Low** (weight 70) — The most common. Wholesale typically stocks **14 lots** of Low-tier guns, with **3–10 units per lot**. You'll almost always find something in this range sitting on a shelf.
- **Medium** (weight 25) — Less common. About **5 lots** per rotation, with **1–5 units per lot**. You might need to check back or ask around.
- **High** (weight 5) — Rare. Usually only **1 lot** per rotation, with **1–2 units**. If you see a High-tier gun in stock, grab it — it probably won't be there next time.

**What this means for you:** If you're looking for a basic sidearm, you can probably walk into any store and find one. If you've got your eye on a top-shelf weapon, be ready to wait, check back often, or talk to a store owner about special ordering.

## Type

Guns are also classified by their damage type: **Power**, **Smart**, or **Tech**.

| Type | What It Is |
|------|-----------|
| **Power** | Conventional firearms. Bullets, gunpowder, kinetic force. Point and shoot. |
| **Smart** | Guided-projectile weapons. These guns use tracking technology to home in on targets. |
| **Tech** | Electromagnetic or charged weapons. They use tech-based mechanisms to deliver damage — think railguns, charge rifles, and similar hardware. |

### How Type Works in the System

Right now, the gun type is mainly a **categorization** — it tells you what kind of weapon you're dealing with and shows up on inventory displays and store listings. It doesn't change how you buy, sell, or interact with the gun through the bot.

Where it *does* matter is in **RP**. The type of gun your character carries says something about them and can affect how scenes play out. A Solo rocking a Power-type assault rifle is a different vibe from a Netrunner packing a Smart pistol.

## Restriction

This is the big one. Every gun has a restriction level that determines **who can buy it and what approvals are needed**. There are three levels:

### Basic

No restrictions at all. Walk into a store, pick your gun, pay for it, walk out. Anyone can buy a Basic weapon — no questions asked, no paperwork needed.

### Controlled

These weapons require **pre-approval** before a store can sell one to you. Specifically, your **character** needs to be on the store's "Cleared for Controlled" list.

**How to get cleared:**
1. Talk to the store owner (in RP or out of RP, depending on how your server does things).
2. The store owner adds your character to their approved buyer list through the Manage Buyers menu.
3. Once your character is on the list, the store can sell Controlled weapons to that character.

**Important:** Approval is tied to your *character*, not your Discord account. If you have a Solo who's been cleared, that doesn't mean your Nomad alt is cleared too. Each character needs their own approval at each store.

### Restricted

The highest restriction level. Buying a Restricted weapon requires **two things**:

1. **Your character must be on the store's approved buyer list** (same as Controlled).
2. **A Fixer or Admin must approve the specific sale.** When the store tries to sell you a Restricted weapon, the bot posts an approval request to the staff channel. A Fixer or Admin has **5 minutes** to react and approve it. If nobody approves in time, the sale doesn't go through.

This means even if you're on the cleared list, a staff member still has to sign off on every single Restricted purchase. It's an extra layer of oversight for the most dangerous weapons in the city.

### The Black Market

There's one more way to get your hands on Controlled and Restricted weapons: **the Black Market**.

Black Market shops have access to the full catalog of Controlled and Restricted weapons — they aren't limited by the weekly wholesale rotation like regular stores. They always have stock available. The catch? You're going to pay a **premium price**. Black Market guns cost significantly more than their standard retail counterparts.

Note that the same approval rules still apply when a Black Market store sells to you. Your character still needs to be on the store's approved buyer list for Controlled weapons, and Restricted sales still require Fixer/Admin approval. The Black Market's advantage is **availability**, not bypassing the approval process.

## Quick Reference

| Property | Values | What to Know |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| **Power Level** | Low / Medium / High | Affects rarity in stores. Low = common, High = rare. |
| **Type** | Power / Smart / Tech | Categorizes the weapon. Mostly matters for RP. |
| **Restriction** | Basic / Controlled / Restricted | Determines what approvals you need to buy. |

### Can I Buy This Gun?

| Restriction | What You Need |
|-------------|--------------|
| Basic | Nothing — just money. |
| Controlled | Your character on the store's approved list. |
| Restricted | Your character on the approved list + a Fixer/Admin approves the sale in real time. |
| Black Market | Same approval rules as above, but stock is always available (at a higher price). |
