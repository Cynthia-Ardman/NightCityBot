**__Gun Store Owners/Sellers__**

** __Upcoming Gun System Changes (Slow Rollout)__**

Hey y’all, heads up that we’re rolling out a **new gun buying/selling system** soon. The goal is simple:

* **Money can’t buy unlimited power** (this isn’t grinder gear-progression RP)
* **Shops stay relevant** (no more “everyone has everything forever so the city feels empty”)
* **Low admin overhead** (simple rules + sheets + seller logs)
### What this means for gun stores
**You become the main point of sale for weapons** in NCRP. More walk-in RP, more special orders, more reasons for players to interact with the city instead of bypassing it with cash.
### The new structure (high level)

**1) __Master Gun Catalog (cyberware-style)__**
* We’ll maintain a master “what exists in NCRP” catalog (like we do for cyberware).
* We'll also maintain a list per character like we do for cyberware
* **Being in the catalog ≠ stocked somewhere.**
* To buy a gun, a player needs a seller (gun store / fixer) who carries it or can special order it.

**2) __Gun Access Tiers__**
* **Common:** cash only (normal shelf stuff)
* **Controlled:** cash + buyer must be on *your* “Cleared for Controlled” list (rep/vouch based per player character)
* **Restricted:** cash + cleared list + **Fixer sponsor approval** (This is mostly going to be story based, meaning they need to have a lore reason to have a weapon)
* **Unique:** plot-only

**3) __Stock vs Special Order__**
* If you have it **stocked**, you can sell it immediately.
* If you **don’t** have it stocked, you can **special order it** → arrives **next week** (this keeps stocking meaningful and stops “instant anything”).

**4) __Seller-Logged Purchases (important)__**
* **If the sale isn’t logged by the seller, it doesn’t count.**
* We'll do this via a form that you'll fill out, just really simple, character name + gun name
* This is how we keep it enforceable and avoid arguments later.

### __What you’ll be asked to do__
We’re going to give you a **simple template sheet** (like cyberware) where you track:
* What guns you *carry*
* Your prices (you set your markup)
* **Quantity tracking only for Controlled/Restricted**
* Your “Cleared for Controlled Weapons” list (rep/vouch/faction/etc per character, but this is up to you **In Character**)
### __Restocking (retailer flow)__
* You’ll buy stock through the **weekly wholesale list** (run by fixers / corp producers / black market sources).
* Wholesale money **doesn’t go to a player’s pocket**.
* You choose your retail pricing.
### __Custom weapons (prefabs not on the list)__
* Players request customs via ticket, either character ticket or general ticket, this goes to fixers, you don't need to worry about that.
* If approved, we assign **tier + price** and add it to the master catalog.
* Then you can sell it as a **special order** like anything else.
## Rollout plan 
We’re doing this in phases:

1. **Immediate / now-ish:** Get feedback from you all.
2. **Next:** collect what guns people already have (via a survey)
3. **Then:** finalize the master gun catalog + start wholesale/retail sales through the system
4. **After that:** inventory + logging becomes “officially required” and the system goes live.

If you’re a gun store owner, your “prep work” is basically:

* Decide what you want to stock + what you’d price it at
* Think about what qualifies someone for your **Controlled cleared list**
* Be ready to use the template sheet + log sales (we’ll give you the format)

This is your chance to give feedback on this before we go live with it, which will be a bit because we need to gather all the weapons we currently have.
